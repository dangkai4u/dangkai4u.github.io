I've always wanted a place of my own — to write tech notes, record ideas, and share useful tools.

<!--more-->

No chasing traffic, no chasing perfection. Just an outlet for my thoughts.

Writing is an extension of thinking. Often I think I've figured something out, but when I start writing, I realize there are still many fuzzy areas. So this blog is mostly for myself. If it happens to help you too, even better.

Topics I'll cover:

- **Tech**: Problems and solutions from work
- **AI Tools**: Hands-on experiences with various AI products
- **Thoughts**: Reflections on reading, work, and life

Let's get started.
